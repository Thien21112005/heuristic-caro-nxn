Mode: Operate (the game and its setup screens). World lends type, palette, density and one signature move; controls stay standard.

THESIS: Caro is what you played in the back of a school notebook, so the app is that page: cool paper, ink, and two ridiculous faces.
OWN-WORLD: A Vietnamese vở ô ly: faint violet-blue squares, a pink-red margin line, purple ballpoint, the teacher's red pen, a yellow highlighter. Not cream, not serif, not binder rings.
STORY: Open the page, circle your choices in pen, play on the grid, and the winning line gets highlighted and looped in red pen with the winner's face beside it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Handwritten "Caro" at the margin with a pen underline, one solid quick-start button naming the last settings, a real mini board with large faces on the right.
FORM: seed: none (unavailable): the user pinned this direction, so no concept roll was made. The pen circle is the only selected state of a choice. Solid violet is reserved for the single main action on a screen. Mali for headings, numbers and buttons; Inter for small body text. Pen-wobble lines come from a seeded Sketch utility, so they never shimmer.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
