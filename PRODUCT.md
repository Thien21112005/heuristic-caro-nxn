# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

Desktop app: Java 17, Swing + FlatLaf, packaged for Windows with jpackage. Not web, iOS or Android.

## Users

Vietnamese students and their friends (the app also ships in English). They play one-handed with a mouse on a laptop,
often in a short break, against the computer, side by side on one machine, or across a shared Wi-Fi network.

## Product Purpose

Caro (Gomoku / Tic-Tac-Toe) on any board from 3×3 to 20×20, with three AI levels, a turn clock, hints, undo, replay,
lifetime statistics and LAN play. Success is getting into a game within seconds and always being able to read the board.

## Brand Personality

Playful, cheeky, schoolyard. The two meme-face pieces are the characters of the game and the reason people remember it.

## Anti-references

- The previous dark neon "cyberpunk" look: wallpaper behind a board, gradient title, glowing lines, violet everywhere.
- Skeuomorphic notebook costume: ring binders, torn edges, coffee stains, ruled-paper backgrounds behind forms.
- Old WinForms grey panels.

## Design Principles

1. The board is the page: nothing competes with the grid and the pieces.
2. One solid action per screen; a choice is shown by circling it, not by another solid button.
3. Faces are the heroes: pieces stay large and uncovered, above all at the moment of winning.
4. Never cover the board: game-end results sit beside it, and the board never jumps size.
5. Everything works from the keyboard, including placing a piece.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text at least 4.5:1 and UI parts at least 3:1 on paper. State is never colour alone. Full Vietnamese diacritics in
every font. Keyboard play with a visible cursor. Must work at the 980×680 minimum window.
